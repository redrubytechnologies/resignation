Employee Resignation Management

Manage the complete resignation workflow — from submission to relieving with proper documentation.

RedRuby Technologies Odoo 16.0

Overview

The Employee Resignation Management module streamlines the employee resignation and exit process. It manages the complete resignation workflow from submission to employee relieving, ensuring proper documentation and compliance with company policies.

Key Features

Resignation Submission Workflow

Employees can submit resignation requests that go through a structured approval process.

Exit Process Management

Track and manage all exit formalities including clearance, handover, and final settlement.

Relieving Letter Generation

Auto-generate professional relieving letters upon resignation approval and completion.

Notice Period Tracking

Monitors the notice period with automatic alerts and reminders for HR and managers.

Screenshots

Place your screenshot images in static/description/screenshots/ folder named screenshot_1.png, screenshot_2.png, screenshot_3.png etc.
